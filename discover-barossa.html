Discover Barossa

Swap the hustle and bustle of Adelaide for a relaxing day in the world-famous Barossa Valley.

This guided small-group tour is the perfect blend of premium wine tastings, gourmet local flavors, and iconic South Australian heritage. From the grand estates of Chateau Yaldara and Seppeltsfield to the boutique charm of Kies Family Wines, every stop is curated to showcase the very best of Australia’s premier wine region.

Tour Highlights

  • 4 Premium Wine Tastings: Sip through handpicked flights at the region’s finest heritage and boutique estates.

  • Gourmet Vineyard Lunch: Enjoy a chef-prepared meal at a celebrated Barossa winery.

  • Iconic Seppeltsfield Estate: Explore the "Crown Jewel" of the Barossa, including the Avenue of Palms, Vasse Virgin, and JamFactory.

  • Sweet Local Treats: Indulge in the famous Barossa Browns Donuts and a visit to the Barossa Valley Chocolate Company.

  • Small-Group Comfort: A personalized journey with a maximum of 24 travelers.

  • Hassle-Free Pick-up: Complimentary CBD hotel transfers within 2km of Adelaide.

What's included

  • All tasting fees at 4 premium wineries.

  • A delicious gourmet winery lunch.

  • Luxury transport in a comfortable, air-conditioned bus.

  • Expert commentary from a professional local guide.

  • Chilled bottled water provided throughout the day.

Itinerary:

Pick-up

  • We offer convenient pick-ups from Adelaide CBD hotels, our central meeting point at Stamford Plaza, or custom arrangements outside the radius. Please provide your hotel name when booking!

Morning

  • 09:45 AM – Chateau Yaldara: Start the day at this majestic 1847 stone estate with a premium wine tasting. 10:30 AM – Seppeltsfield Heritage Estate: Journey down the legendary Avenue of Palms. Spend 40 minutes exploring the Artisan Village, including Vasse Virgin skincare and JamFactory craft studios.

Midday

  • 12:00 PM – Barossa Browns Donuts: A 10-minute pitstop for the valley's most famous handcrafted donuts (Available Wed–Sat). 12:30 PM – Hemera Estate: Discover bold, boutique reds during a 40-minute guided tasting. 01:15 PM – Kies Family Wines: Settle in for 90 minutes. Enjoy a premium wine flight followed by a gourmet regional lunch at their vineyard café.

Afternoon

  • Scenic drive past the Jacob’s Creek Trail of Discovery. 03:15 PM – Barossa Valley Chocolate Company: A 10-minute indulgent stop for artisan chocolates. 03:45 PM – Ubertas Wines: A 40-minute tasting of minimal-intervention wines in a modern, scenic setting. Return: Scenic drive back to Adelaide city.

Important Information

  • Duration: Approx. 8 hours

  • Ages: 0–80

  • Group size: Maximum 24 travelers

  • Note: Not recommended for pregnant travellers or those with back problems
