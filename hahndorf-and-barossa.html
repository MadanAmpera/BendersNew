Ultimate Hahndorf & Barossa Valley Day Tour: Wine and Culture

Experience the best of South Australia’s two most iconic regions in one unforgettable day. This all-inclusive small-group tour takes you from the rolling greenery of the Adelaide Hills to the world-renowned vineyards of the Barossa Valley. Indulge in strawberry picking, historic village charm, artisan chocolates, and premium wine tastings for the ultimate local experience.

Tour Highlights:

  • Beerenberg Farm: Pick your own seasonal strawberries or browse the famous farm shop.

  • Hahndorf Village: Explore Australia’s oldest German settlement and its artisan boutiques.

  • Melba’s Chocolate Factory: Discover nostalgic sweets and traditional chocolate making.

  • Kies Family Wines: Enjoy a premium boutique wine tasting and a gourmet vineyard lunch.

  • Seppeltsfield Heritage Estate: Visit the Barossa’s most iconic estate and artisan village.

  • Ubertas Wines: End the day with a refined tasting at a boutique, family-run winery.

  • Small-Group Experience: Travel in comfort with a maximum of 11 to 24 guests.

What's included

  • All wine tasting fees and lunch.

  • Round-trip transportation in a comfortable, air-conditioned bus.

  • Entry to Beerenberg Farm (picking is seasonal).

  • Chilled bottled water for every guest.

  • Expert English-speaking guide with local stories and insights.

Tour Itinerary

Morning: Adelaide Hills Treasures

  • 09:45 AM – Beerenberg Farm: Start your day at this world-famous family farm. During strawberry season, enjoy strawberry picking (Entry included; strawberries are charged by weight at $12.50/kg ). Outside of picking season, explore the incredible Farm Shop filled with heritage jams and chutneys.

  • 10:30 AM – Hahndorf Historic Village: Step into a storybook as you wander the 19th-century streets of Australia’s oldest German settlement. Discover artisan craft shops, traditional bakeries, and quaint cafes during your 75-minute visit.

  • 12:15 PM – Melba’s Chocolate & Confectionery: A nostalgic stop in Woodside to see traditional sweets being made using heritage machinery. Perfect for picking up some handmade treats for the road!

Midday: Barossa Flavors & Gourmet Lunch

  • 01:15 PM – Kies Family Wines: We cross into the Barossa Valley for a signature experience at this family-owned gem. Enjoy a guided flight of estate-grown wines followed by a gourmet regional lunch at the Monkey Nut Café, overlooking the peaceful vines.

Afternoon: Heritage & Boutique Vines

  • 02:50 PM – Seppeltsfield Heritage Estate: Experience the majesty of the "Avenue of Palms." Spend 40 minutes exploring the JamFactory craft studios, Vasse Virgin skincare, and the Octeine Cafe. (Please note: This stop is for exploring the historic grounds and artisan village).

  • 03:40 PM – Ubertas Wines: Our final destination is this boutique winery known for its intimate atmosphere and minimal-intervention wines. Savor a premium tasting that captures the true essence of Barossa terroir.

Return (approx. 04:30 PM Departure) Reflect on a day of indulgence and culture as we take the scenic route back to Adelaide, arriving at approximately 05:30 PM.

Important Information

  • Duration: Approx. 8 hours

  • Pick-up: Complimentary CBD transfers available. Please provide your hotel name when booking.

  • Group size: Intimate small-group experience.

  • Note: Itinerary order and venues may be subject to change based on availability to ensure the best possible guest experience.
