About Adelaide Hills

Just an hour's drive from Adelaide, you’ll find yourself meandering through picturesque, tree-lined roads as the renowned wineries of the Adelaide Hills invite you to sample their cool-climate wines. With Bender’s Bus Tour, you’ll uncover some of South Australia’s best wine, food and views. Each tour introduces you to a region where winemakers craft bold and unique wines that reflect their passion.

The beauty of the Adelaide Hills lies not only in its wines but also in its diverse landscape. Winding roads, rolling hills, and deep valleys create distinct microclimates, inspiring the region's artisans, chefs, and winemakers alike. Though young, the Adelaide Hills has quickly established itself as a key player in shaping modern Australian wine.

Home to over 90 wine labels and 50 cellar doors, the Adelaide Hills is Australia’s leading cool-climate wine region, internationally celebrated for its premium wines and stunning scenery.

Nestled in the Mount Lofty Ranges, just east of Adelaide, the region stretches across 70 kilometers, with its highest vineyards sitting at altitudes of 600-650 meters in areas like Crafers, Summertown, Piccadilly, and Carey Gully. As one of the largest and most diverse wine regions in Australia, the Adelaide Hills promises an unforgettable experience for wine lovers.
