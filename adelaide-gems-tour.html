Adelaide Gems in a Day: Hahndorf, Glenelg Beach & City Highlights

Think you can't see the best of Adelaide in a single day? Think again. This tour packs in everything that makes our city so special — golden beaches, vibrant markets, stunning gardens, panoramic hilltop views, and the charm of Australia's oldest surviving German village — all in one incredible 6.5-hour journey.

Whether you're a first-time visitor or a local looking at Adelaide through fresh eyes, the Adelaide Gems in a Day tour is the best way to experience it all without the stress of driving, parking, or planning.

Tour Itinerary

  • Glenelg Beach: Your day kicks off at the iconic Glenelg Beach — Adelaide's favourite coastal escape. With 45 minutes to soak up the sea air, stroll along the foreshore, or grab a coffee by the water, it's the perfect way to start the day. Plenty of photography opportunities too!

  • Adelaide City Highlights: As we wind through the city, take in some of Adelaide's most iconic landmarks on a scenic drive past the magnificent Adelaide Oval, the historic St Peter's Cathedral, and the beautiful tree-lined North Terrace. A great introduction to the heart of our city.

  • National War Memorial: A short but meaningful 15-minute stop to pay respects at one of Adelaide's most significant landmarks — a powerful reminder of the stories that shaped this great nation.

  • State Library of South Australia: Spend 30 minutes exploring one of Adelaide's most beautiful heritage buildings. Whether you're an architecture lover or a history buff, this is a stop that never disappoints.

  • Adelaide Central Market: One full hour in Adelaide's beating heart. The Adelaide Central Market is a food lover's paradise, brimming with fresh produce, artisan goods, international flavours, and some of the best coffee in the city. Browse, taste, and take in the incredible atmosphere of one of the Southern Hemisphere's largest and most diverse fresh food markets.

  • Palm House – Adelaide Botanic Garden: A 30-minute escape into one of Adelaide's most beloved green spaces. The stunning Palm House is a Victorian-era glasshouse surrounded by magnificent gardens — a wonderful spot to slow down, breathe in, and appreciate the natural beauty Adelaide has to offer.

  • Mount Lofty Summit: No visit to Adelaide is complete without taking in the breathtaking panoramic views from Mount Lofty Summit. With 15 minutes at the top, you'll see why this is one of the most photographed spots in South Australia. On a clear day, the views stretch all the way across the city to the coast — absolutely stunning.

  • Hahndorf: Save the best for last! Spend 1.5 hours wandering the charming main street of Hahndorf — Australia's oldest surviving German settlement nestled in the beautiful Adelaide Hills. Browse boutique shops, galleries, and cafés, sample local produce, and soak up the rich history and culture of this truly unique village.

What's Included

  • 6.5 hours of guided sightseeing across Adelaide.

  • Complimentary pick-up and drop-off from Adelaide CBD and Glenelg hotels.

  • All stops listed above.

  • Photography opportunities throughout the day.
